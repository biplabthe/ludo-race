Privacy Policy — Ludo Race

Ludo Race is a free online board game. This page explains what information the game uses.

What we collect

When you sign in with Facebook or Google, the game receives your public profile name and profile picture. Guest players only provide a name they type. This is used only to show your name and picture to the people you play with.

What we store

While you are online we store your name, picture and whether you are free or playing, so friends can invite you. Game rooms store the players and the game board. Your online status is removed automatically when you leave the site.

What we don't do

We do not sell or share your information, show ads, or post anything to your Facebook account.

Data deletion

Use the "लॉगआउट" (logout) button in the game to remove your online status. To delete everything linked to your account, contact the game owner through the contact details on the Facebook app page, and it will be deleted within 30 days. You can also remove the app from your Facebook settings under Apps and Websites.
